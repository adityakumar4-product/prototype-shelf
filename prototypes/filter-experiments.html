About this treatment
Filter users convert at 2× non-filter users, but filters sit deep inside All Courses. A glass Filters button docks above the bottom nav once the header scrolls away, and Teachers joins the filters. One glass dock above the bottom nav holds Search on the left and Filters on the right, so both high-converting actions share a single floating spot. Search docks as a glass pill on the left; Filters is its own round glass button on the right. Each keeps a distinct target and a distinct job.
